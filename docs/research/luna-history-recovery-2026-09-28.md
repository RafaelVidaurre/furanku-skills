## What to do with this evidence

Before collecting more sessions, make the assessment result preserve these distinct
facts mechanically:

1. Original task and continuation identity, with dated authorized scope changes.
2. Original requested outcomes proved, unproved or reassigned; cause can remain mixed.
3. Final deliverable quality under the final scope.
4. Source-supported worker errors, corrections, coordinator interventions and overruns.
5. Resource observations and their attribution limits.

This review records those facts privately. Production ingestion and automatic
calibration of these fields remain unimplemented; this document does not activate
them. A narrowed handoff with score 3 must retain its unfinished original goal.
Do not turn null quality into missing process evidence when concrete mistakes exist.

The immediate evidence gap is a comparable task with equivalent tools, authority
and attributable resources—not more unrelated Sol sessions. Keep the current
manual routing judgment and use these observations as caution about supervision
cost, not as an automatic penalty against every Luna domain.
